Blocked Account Guide: Choose Your Provider

A blocked Sperrkonto is mandatory for your German student visa. Here is a comparison of the 3 main providers.

🏦 Kotak Mahindra Bank

Free setup
  • No account opening fee
  • Easy INR to EUR transfers from India
  • Allows lump sum withdrawal in Germany
  • Processing time: 5–7 business days
  • Popular choice for Indian students
Fastest

⚡ Expatrio

€89 setup
  • Account ready in less than 24 hours
  • Bundled with TK health insurance
  • 100% online application
  • Free German current account included
  • Excellent English customer support

🛡️ Fintiba

€159 setup
  • German IBAN and deposit protection
  • Setup in about 10 minutes
  • Includes DAK/MAWISTA health insurance
  • Mature platform, widely trusted
  • Integrated insurance + blocked account

Proof of funds required: €11,904 per year (as of 2025). This must be deposited before your visa interview.
