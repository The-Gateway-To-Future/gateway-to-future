Post-Arrival Support: Settle in Germany Without Stress

Our commitment does not end when your visa is approved. We help you navigate the first critical weeks after landing in Germany.

🏠

Housing & Accommodation

Assistance finding shared apartments (WG), student housing (Studentenwerk), or temporary studio flats in Berlin and other university cities.

📍

Anmeldung (City Registration)

Step-by-step guidelines to book your local Bürgeramt registration appointment and format your landlord confirmation letter.

🏦

German Bank Account

Guidance on opening a local current account (Girokonto) to receive your monthly blocked account payouts and pay rent.

🩺

Health Insurance Registration

Enrollment in TK, AOK, or DAK health insurance schemes to satisfy German university matriculation requirements.

🛫

Airport Pick-Up & SIM Card

Practical arrival orientations, public transport navigation assistance, and helping you buy a local mobile SIM card.

📄

Residence Permit (Aufenthaltstitel)

Pre-compiling documents to extend your entry visa into a student or vocational training residence permit at the local Ausländerbehörde.
