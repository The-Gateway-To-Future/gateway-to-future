About Us

Your Gateway to Germany begins here.

Who We Are

Built for Indian learners planning a serious future in Germany.

Gateway to Future is a premium German language education platform based in Berlin, dedicated to helping Indian learners achieve their dreams of studying and working in Germany.

Our courses are CEFR-aligned, covering levels A1 through C1, and are designed by experienced German language teachers who understand the unique challenges faced by Indian students.

Berlin-based A1-C1

German language training, pathway support, and student mentorship.

Our Mission

Make German learning accessible, affordable, and effective.

We believe that learning German should be accessible, affordable, and effective. Our mission is to provide high-quality language materials and personalized guidance to help you succeed in Germany.

Why Choose Us?

A focused support system for your Germany pathway.

  • CEFR-aligned curriculum (A1 to C1)
  • Hindi and English support
  • Focus on Telc and Goethe certification
  • Based in Berlin, Germany
  • Affordable pricing for Indian students

Ashutosh Sharma

Founder & German Pathway Expert
Deutsch

Ashutosh Sharma ist der Gründer von Gateway to Future und ein erfahrener Berater für Bildungs- und Karrierewege in Deutschland. Nach seinem Medizinstudium an der Lugansk Medical State University absolvierte er eine Ausbildung zum Pflegefachmann (Psychiatrie) und arbeitete als Pflegehelfer in Berlin. Mit seinen umfassenden Kenntnissen des deutschen Bildungs- und Gesundheitssystems und Sprachkenntnissen in Deutsch (B2), Englisch (B2), Hindi und Urdu unterstützt er Studentinnen und Studenten kompetent bei Visaverfahren, Studienkolleg-Bewerbungen und dem gesamten Umsiedlungsprozess.

English

Ashutosh Sharma is the founder of Gateway to Future and an expert advisor for academic and career pathways in Germany. Following his medical studies at Lugansk Medical State University, he completed training as a healthcare professional (psychiatry) and worked as a care assistant in Berlin.
