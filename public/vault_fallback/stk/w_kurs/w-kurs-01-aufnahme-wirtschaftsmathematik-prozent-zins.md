## 📐 Mathematische & Ökonomische Formeln
- `K_n = K_0 \cdot (1 + \frac{p}{100})^n`
- `Z = \frac{K \cdot p \cdot t}{100 \cdot 360}`
- `K_0 = \frac{K_n}{(1 + i)^n} = K_n \cdot (1 + i)^{-n}`
