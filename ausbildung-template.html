Healthcare & Nursing Vocation

Generalist Nurse (Pflegefachmann/Pflegefachfrau)

Germany's healthcare sector requires over 150,000 certified nurses by 2030. The generalist training enables you to work in hospitals, pediatric units, outpatient care, or senior clinics across Europe.

Vocation Overview

A Generalist Nurse (Pflegefachmann) works in close cooperation with physicians, operating theater technicians, and clinical administrators. The Ausbildung curriculum was unified in 2020, merging geriatric care, pediatric care, and general nursing into a single, high-status national qualification recognized across all EU member states.

Key Responsibilities

  • Patient Monitoring: Checking vital signs, updating clinical records, and executing diagnostic procedures.
  • Medication & Care: Administering prescribed injections, managing IVs, and assisting with post-surgery wound care.
  • Assisting Physicians: Prepping patients for surgical operations and coordinating patient releases.
  • Patient Counselling: Advising families on home care systems and managing emergency protocols.

Prerequisites & Qualification Standards

To qualify for the Generalist Nurse Ausbildung and get your visa approved in India, you must meet the following federal requirements:

  • Education: Minimum 10th or 12th standard certificate (from a recognized state board) with decent grades in science and languages.
  • Language Target: **Goethe/telc B2 German certificate** is mandatory before embassy submission. Since you will interact with patients, clinical documentation, and German doctors, high proficiency is required.
  • Personal Suitability: Medical certificate showing physical health clearance, and a clean police clearance record (PCC).

Stipend Progression & Benefits

Nursing is one of the highest-paying Ausbildung programs in Germany. You are paid a monthly wage directly by your training provider from day one, which increases every year:

1st Year Stipend (Training phase) €1,150 – €1,200 / Month
2nd Year Stipend (Intermediate phase) €1,200 – €1,300 / Month
3rd Year Stipend (Senior phase) €1,300 – €1,650 / Month

Post-Graduation Career Progression

Upon passing the state examination, you are guaranteed employment. The starting salary for a certified nurse in Germany (Pflegefachkraft) ranges from **€2,800 to €3,500 gross per month** (plus Sunday, night-shift, and holiday bonuses). Within two years of working, you qualify for permanent residency in Germany.

Vocation Summary

Duration: 3 Years
Intake: April / October
Prerequisite: 12th Std + B2 German
Starting Stipend: €1,150/Month
Employment: 100% Guaranteed
PR Path: 2 Years post-grad
